# From Green Tea to Targeted Therapy: Integrating EGCG into Modern Oncology

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains the research poster presented at the ICABB 2026 by students of the Department of Biotechnology, Jaypee Institute of Information Technology.

The poster explores the anticancer potential of **Epigallocatechin Gallate (EGCG)** — a major bioactive catechin found in green tea — and its role in modern oncology through molecular targeting, apoptosis induction, and inhibition of cancer progression pathways.

## 🎯 Aim of the Study

This poster reviews published studies highlighting the anticancer potential of EGCG from green tea, focusing on:

* Cancer prevention mechanisms
* Molecular pathways targeted by EGCG
* Therapeutic and adjuvant applications in oncology

---

## 🔬 Key Highlights

* EGCG demonstrates strong **antioxidant**, **anti-inflammatory**, and **anticancer** properties.
* Targets multiple signaling pathways including:

  * PI3K/Akt/mTOR
  * MAPK/ERK
  * NF-κB
* Promotes apoptosis through mitochondrial pathways.
* Inhibits:

  * Angiogenesis
  * Metastasis
  * Tumor proliferation
* Shows promise as a supportive agent alongside conventional cancer therapies.
